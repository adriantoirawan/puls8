# 🚀 Tour de Puls8: The Interactive Guide

Welcome to **Puls8**, the ultimate Hacktiv8 classroom management platform! This platform was built to solve the real, grueling problems of running a coding bootcamp: scattered grades, tedious cohort assignments, and students suffering alone at 3 AM.

This guide will take you on a hands-on journey through every mechanic, edge case, and safeguard we built. 

---

## 🎭 Act I: The Onboarding Experience
*First, let's see how Puls8 handles the front door.*

### Step 1: The Bad 
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/register`.
2. Purposely try to break the app. Enter an empty email and a password.
3. **What You'll See:** Instead of the server crashing and dumping a raw Sequelize error to the screen, you will be smoothly redirected back to the form via the URL `?error=...`. A beautiful red Bootstrap alert will say: *"Validation error: Validation notEmpty on email failed"*. This is our **Post/Redirect/Get (PRG) Pattern** protecting against double-submissions.

### Step 2: The Good
1. Now, enter a valid email (e.g., `newstudent@gmail.com`) and a secure password.
2. Hit **Register**.
3. **What You'll See:** You are cleanly redirected to `/login`. Behind the scenes, a Sequelize `beforeCreate` hook just hashed your password using `bcryptjs`!
4. Login with your new credentials. You are now in the Student Dashboard!

---

## 🛡️ Act II: The Student Profile Constraint
*Let's set up your profile, and observe a strict business logic constraint.*

### Step 3: Edit Profile
1. Click the **"Edit My Profile"** button on your dashboard.
2. Look at the `Learning Style` and `Grit Level` fields.
3. **What You'll See:** They are completely grayed out (Read-Only). 
4. **Why?** Because Grit and Learning Style are subjective *Instructor Evaluations*, not self-reported preferences! A student cannot hack their own grit level to a 10.
5. Enter a real Discord Handle into the **Discord Handle** field (e.g., `@hacker_pro_42`) and click Save. Note: We recently migrated from messy 18-digit Snowflake IDs to human-readable handles!

---

## 👨‍🏫 Act III: The Instructor Command Center
*Time to put on the Instructor hat.*

### Step 4: Curriculum Building
1. Open an Incognito window (or logout) and navigate to `http://localhost:3000/login`.
2. Login as an instructor (e.g., `instructor1@puls8.com`).
3. Click **"+ New Class"** at the top right. 
4. **The Bad:** Try creating a class with a Phase Level of `5`. The form strictly enforces `max="3"`.
5. **The Good:** Create a class named "Fox" with Phase Level `1`. 

### Step 5: The Waiting Pool
1. Look at the bottom of the Instructor Dashboard. You will see a yellow table labeled **"Waiting Pool (Unassigned Students)"**.
2. **What You'll See:** The `newstudent@gmail.com` account you created in Act I is sitting here! Because they just registered, they have no cohort.
3. Select your newly created "Fox" class from the dropdown and click **Assign**.
4. Poof! They are officially in your cohort.

### Step 6: The Search Engine & Empty States
1. At the top of the dashboard, you'll see a search bar. Type in a gibberish name like `wadidaw` and hit Search.
2. **What You'll See:** Instead of showing broken, empty class tables, the system dynamically filters the classes entirely. You'll see a clean blue alert: *"No students found matching your search term wadidaw"*.
3. Try searching for `puls8.com`. You'll instantly see all the procedural data generated by our massive seeder!

---

## 🧠 Act IV: The AI Dossier & Evaluations
*Let's generate some insights.*

### Step 7: The Evaluation Room
1. Find your new student in the main dashboard table and click the blue **"Evaluate"** button.
2. Now YOU have the power! Set their Learning Style to "Visual" and their Grit Level to an "8". Click Save.
3. **What You'll See:** Look at the **Dossier** column on the dashboard. It dynamically reads: *"Student newstudent@gmail.com (Phase 1) - Discord: @hacker_pro_42, Style: Visual, Grit: 8/5"*. This is generated via a custom Sequelize Instance Method!

---

## 📝 Act V: The Grade Book
*Time to issue some grades, safely.*

### Step 8: Phase-Locked Grading
1. Click the yellow **"Grade"** button next to your student.
2. Click the **"Select Task"** dropdown.
3. **What You'll See:** You will ONLY see tasks assigned to Phase 1. 
4. **Why?** We implemented a strict Phase Filter. It is impossible for an instructor to accidentally grade a Phase 1 student on a Phase 3 React Native task!
5. Select a task, give them an `85`, and submit. Behind the scenes, Puls8 runs an intelligent `findOrCreate` upsert so it never creates duplicate score rows.

---

## 🚨 Act VI: The Rescue Flare (P2P Support)
*The most advanced feature in Puls8.*

### Step 9: The Paradox Filter
1. Go back to your Student browser window (or login as the student again).
2. Click the orange **"Deploy Rescue Flare"** button.
3. Open the "Select Task" dropdown.
4. **What You'll See:** The task you just scored an `85` on is MISSING!
5. **Why?** Our anti-spam logic prevents students from deploying a Rescue Flare for a task they already passed.

### Step 10: Firing the Flare
1. Select a task you *haven't* passed yet and hit **Deploy Rescue Flare!**
2. **Behind the scenes:** Puls8 queries the database for another student in your exact same class who scored an 80 or higher on this specific task (a "Smart Peer"). Crucially, it excludes *you* from the query, preventing a paradox where the bot asks you to rescue yourself.
3. The server fires a webhook to Discord, tagging the Smart Peer's human-readable handle, rallying them to your aid in real-time!

---

## ⚖️ Act VII: Phase Resolution (The End of the Line)
*The hardest part of an instructor's job: moving students up, or holding them back.*

### Step 11: The Hard Choices
1. Go back to the Instructor Dashboard.
2. Next to your student, you have two final buttons: **Move Up** and **Repeat**.

**Scenario A: "Move Up"**
- Click it. 
- **What Happens:** The student's Phase Level increments (e.g., Phase 1 -> Phase 2). Their `classId` is wiped, dumping them back into the Waiting Pool so they can be assigned to a new Phase 2 cohort.

**Scenario B: "Repeat"**
- Click it.
- **What Happens:** Their Phase Level stays the same. They are flagged as an official repeater (`isRepeater = true`). Their `classId` is wiped. AND MOST IMPORTANTLY: Every single one of their previous scores is permanently destroyed using a cascading Promise Chain, giving them a completely clean slate for their repeat phase.
